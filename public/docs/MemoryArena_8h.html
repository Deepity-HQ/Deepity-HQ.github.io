Detailed Description

A 64-byte-aligned bump-pointer allocator over a single heap-allocated block, used to pack every layer's beliefs/errors/ weights/scratch buffers into one contiguous region for cache locality. Host-side counterpart to DeviceMemoryArena.h's CUDA version.

Note
Individual chunks handed out by Allocate() cannot be freed independently; the entire arena is released at once in the destructor.
