Declares the Google Highway (github.com/google/highway) runtime- dispatched implementations backing a handful of CPUBackend's elementwise kernels. Deliberately a separate CMake target (DeepityHighwayKernels, see CMakeLists.txt) compiled WITHOUT the project's hard-pinned -march flag – Highway's own foreach_target.h mechanism compiles multiple per-ISA-target variants of CPUBackendHighway.cpp and picks the best one at runtime; a project-wide fixed ISA would defeat that entirely. Detailed Description

Declares the Google Highway (github.com/google/highway) runtime- dispatched implementations backing a handful of CPUBackend's elementwise kernels. Deliberately a separate CMake target (DeepityHighwayKernels, see CMakeLists.txt) compiled WITHOUT the project's hard-pinned -march flag – Highway's own foreach_target.h mechanism compiles multiple per-ISA-target variants of CPUBackendHighway.cpp and picks the best one at runtime; a project-wide fixed ISA would defeat that entirely.
